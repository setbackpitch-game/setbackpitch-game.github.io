Privacy Policy

Setback! A Pitch Card Game · Effective October 29, 2026

The short version. Solo games stay on your phone. To play with friends online, the app gives you a random player ID and saves your display name, the tables you play at, and the quick-chat lines you send. If the app crashes, it saves a short error report with no name or ID attached. We don't sell your data. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

This policy covers the Android app Setback! A Pitch Card Game (the "app"), published by Setback Pitch Game ("we", "us"). If you have questions, email CONTACT_EMAIL.

What stays on your phone

Everything about solo play is saved only on your device: the game in progress, your display name, and settings like sound and table theme. None of it is sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

What we collect for online play

Playing with friends needs a server so everyone at the table sees the same game. We use Google Firebase for this.

Like any internet service, Firebase receives your device's IP address when the app connects. We don't store it or use it to identify you. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy and security terms.

If you choose to link a Google account (optional), Firebase also stores the email address of that Google account so you can sign in on a new phone. Linking is never required.

Ads and purchases

When a version of the app with ads is released, this section applies to it.

  • Ads. The app shows ads through Google AdMob: one full-screen ad between games and a banner on the home screen, never during a hand. To show and measure ads, AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address, not GPS), and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings, under Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  • Remove Ads purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We receive a record that the purchase happened so we can turn ads off and restore the purchase on a new phone.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't collect your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera.
  • We don't use your data for anything except running and fixing the game, and, once ads are added, showing ads.

Who can see your data

Other players at your tables see your display name, your moves and your quick-chat lines. Google provides our servers (Firebase) and, once added, ads (AdMob) and payments (Google Play). We may disclose data if required by law.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings > Delete my account. This deletes your display name and player ID and closes the tables you host. Records at other people's tables lose any link to your name and are removed when those tables close or expire (within 30 days). Full instructions, including how to ask by email if you no longer have the app, are on the account deletion page.

Security

Data travels to Firebase over an encrypted connection and is stored on Google's servers. Access rules stop players from reading or changing anything except the tables they're part of and their own name. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we collect to a minimum.

Children

The app is meant for players 13 and older and isn't directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or another US state with a privacy law), you may have the right to know what we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, or to delete it. Email us with your player ID (shown in the app under Settings) and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what we collect, we'll say so in the app's update notes.
