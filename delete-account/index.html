Delete your account

Setback! A Pitch Card Game · by Setback Pitch Game

You can delete your Setback! account and its data at any time. Solo play doesn't use an account, so there's nothing to delete for it beyond uninstalling the app.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Setback! and tap Settings on the home screen.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete my account.
  3. Tap it again to confirm.

It takes effect right away. You need to be online.

By email (if you no longer have the app)

Email CONTACT_EMAIL with the subject "Delete my Setback! account". Include your player ID if you have it (shown under Settings in the app), or else your display name and roughly when you last played. We'll delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your display name
  • Your player ID (account), and the linked Google account email if you added one
  • Tables you host, with their quick-chat messages

What's kept, and for how long

  • Tables hosted by other players still contain your moves under your old player ID, which no longer links to any name. They're deleted when the host closes the table, or automatically 30 days after its last move.
  • Error reports never contained your name or ID, so they can't be traced to you. They delete automatically after 90 days.
  • Remove Ads purchase records are kept by Google Play under Google's own policies, so you can restore the purchase later.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

  • A table: as host, open the table menu and tap Close table for everyone, or use Manage under Your tables on the Play with friends screen.
  • Your display name: change it any time on the home screen. Your old name is replaced.
  • Anything else: email us and tell us what you'd like removed.
